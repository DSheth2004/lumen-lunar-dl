## The failure and the near miss

### `S3_ilmenite_err` — a substantive disagreement

Worst-case ilmenite error against the LSCC 20–45 µm lab values, in percentage points of
crystalline-renormalised abundance, against the paper's stated bound of 10:

| sampler | worst error (15071 excepted) |
|---|---|
| converged (`rw`, Nsteps=100) | **17.56** (12001) |
| released (paper's own settings) | **12.89** (12030) |
| Dirichlet prior alone | 10.87 |

It fails under both samplers, so it is not an artefact of the D7 correction. Fit quality
is not the cause: R² is 0.983–0.994, at or above the published values. This is
non-uniqueness: pyroxene is systematically under-predicted and ilmenite over-predicted,
the two trading against each other as the dark, spectrally competing phases. See
`figures/fig04_abundances_vs_lscc.png`. Both configurations use the authors' released
optical-constant library (deviation D3).

### `S3_rms_all` — near miss by 0.0003

The minimum median accepted-model R² is 0.9797 against a floor the text states as 0.98,
driven by soil 71061. Rounded to the two decimals the paper uses, it meets the floor; it is
reported as a near miss, and the tolerance in `paper_targets.csv` is unchanged.

## The main finding — D12

Two of the paper's three stated accuracy claims are satisfied by the **Dirichlet prior
alone**, with the spectra never consulted: olivine to 1.76 points (bound 7), better than
either sampler (5.78 converged, 7.37 released), and plagioclase to 16.92 (bound 20–30), level
with the converged sampler (16.83) and better than the released one (22.56). The olivine
prior mean renormalises to 6.89 %, and the LSCC mare soils hold 7.7–8.7 %.

So the claims that pass are too loose to distinguish the posterior from its prior, and the
one category where the data must do the work (ilmenite) is the one that fails. Together
with D7 (a sampler that does not mix stays near its prior), this is the reproduction's
central result.

Reproduced by `tables/step07_prior_baseline.csv`, fixed seed, 200 000 draws.

## Out of scope

- **Figure 6(i),(j) comparison population.** The panels are drawn against LSCC mare *soil*
  bulk chemistry, not the Apollo mare *basalt* compilation (ApolloBasaltDB, Cone et al.
  2020) the paper uses. The inverted ROIs land outside the mare-soil range in the direction
  the ilmenite over-prediction predicts, which contradicts the paper's claim of overlap;
  because the populations differ, this is reported as suggestive, not conclusive (D14).
- **Figure 5(a)** — the LROC WAC context mosaic. External imagery carrying no reproduced
  quantity.
- **Thermal correction.** M³ L2 is used as delivered, with the standard PDS thermal
  correction (`THERMAL_CORRECTION_FLAG = "Y"`). The Li & Milliken (2016) correction the paper
  names in its Figure 1 caption is not applied. The two differ mainly beyond ~2 µm, which
  lies inside the 0.55–2.50 µm unmixing window, so the M³ mineralogies carry this difference.
- **`F6c_r2_mode` is −1.59.** The marginal-mode estimator takes the mode of each parameter
  separately; the combination need not be a coherent composition, and here it fits worse
  than a constant. This is why the mode-based R² targets are reported, not gated.
- **The ROI selection.** The paper's fresh and weathered pixels are hand-picked (the red and
  blue circles of its Figure 5) and cannot be recovered exactly. The orbital arithmetic is
  therefore gated on the paper's own published band depths; our percentile-based selection
  is reported, never gated (D15).

## Practical notes

- The four soils enter as **specific RELAB spectra**, all **20–45 µm**:
  12001=`c1lr10`, 12030=`c1lr32`, 15071=`c1lr20`, 71061=`c1lr57`. The `c1lr152/154/156/158`
  files in the released MATLAB are the fresh basalts 15058/15555/70017/70035 behind `d_dc`.
- Posterior arrays in `step05_posteriors.npz` are `[9 grain sizes | 9 mass fractions]`; the
  abundances are the **second** half.
- Geometry is **i = 30°, e = 0°, g = 30°** for both the RELAB spectra and M³ L2.
- The M³ strips are 1.8–3.1 GB, but `RECORD_BYTES` is exactly one image line, so
  `m3_fetch.py` downloads only the ROI lines by HTTP range request (~5 MB).
- `data/` is SHA-256 guarded and re-verified at the start **and end** of every stage;
  writing any file into `data/` during a run aborts it.
